## Who we are

- **Name:** Zorixx Consultech Private Limited ("Zorixx"). Positioning line: AI-First Global Consultech.
- **What we do, in one sentence:** Cybersecurity, regulatory compliance and technology risk advisory for regulated financial institutions.
- **Who we are writing for:** compliance heads, CISOs, CTOs and principal officers at Indian stock brokers, NBFCs, fintechs, banks and other SEBI/RBI/IRDAI-regulated entities; for DPDP, also IT/SaaS, e-commerce and digital platforms.
- **What we sell:** system audits, ITGC reviews, SEBI CSCRF and cyber audits, VAPT, DPDP readiness, governance/risk/internal audit, AI governance, ERP and technology controls, digital accessibility audits.

## What we sound like

- **Tone:** calm, precise, practical. An auditor who explains, not a vendor who alarms.
- **Spelling:** Indian/British English (organisation, prioritise, lakh, crore).
- **Words we use:** trust, assurance, controls, evidence, remediation, readiness, resilience, risk-based, regulated, "from compliance to digital trust".
- **Words we never use:** guaranteed, 100% secure, hack-proof, "fully compliant" as a promise, fear-mongering about breaches.
- **Sentence length:** mixed; short lines for the hook, fuller sentences for the explanation.
- **Swearing:** no.
- **Emoji:** rarely, at most one functional emoji (e.g. a checkmark in a list). TODO: confirm.
- **Contractions:** yes in posts, fewer on the page overview.
- **Signature lines:** "Building trust. Strengthening business." / "Decode the risk. Lead with confidence." / "Let's discuss your next audit."

## Our positions

1. Governance should enable progress rather than become a barrier to it.
2. Trust should be designed into every decision, system and process, not added afterwards.
3. Compliance means controls that actually work, not paperwork that passes a review.
4. Finding gaps is half the job; remediation and validation are where the value is.
5. AI adoption needs security and accountability from the first deployment, not after the incident.
