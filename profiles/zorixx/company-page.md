## 2. Overview / About (2,000 characters max)

The first two lines are all most people read before "see more", so they name
the reader and the problem.

```
Regulators now expect stock brokers, NBFCs, fintechs and banks to prove their controls work, not only document them. Zorixx Consultech takes you from system audit to remediation sign-off.

We are an AI-first consultech firm focused on cybersecurity, regulatory compliance and technology risk for regulated financial institutions. Our team turns SEBI, RBI, IRDAI, NPCI and ISO 27001 expectations into audits and controls that stand up to scrutiny.

What we do
• Regulatory audits and assurance: system audits, ITGC reviews, SEBI CSCRF and cyber audits
• Cybersecurity and VAPT: web, mobile, API, network and cloud testing, with retesting
• Data privacy and DPDP: data mapping, gap assessments, notice and consent reviews, breach readiness
• Governance, risk and internal audit: technology risk assessments, control reviews, remediation tracking
• AI governance and assurance: AI risk assessment and responsible AI controls
• ERP and technology controls: access management, segregation of duties, enterprise system reviews

How we work
Understand, assess, prioritise, strengthen, assure, sustain. Every finding is scored on likelihood and impact, so remediation starts where your risk is highest.

Why Zorixx
• Empanelled system auditor with NSE, BSE, MSE, MCX and NCDEX
• Cybersecurity delivered with our partner Ownzap Infosec Pvt. Ltd., a CERT-In-empanelled firm
• One team across privacy, cybersecurity and IT audit, so you don't manage three vendors for one audit cycle

Read Decode by Zorixx, our monthly briefing on AI, cybersecurity, data privacy and compliance.

Let's discuss your next audit.
info@zorixx.ai | +91 70288 61588 | www.zorixx.ai
```

**CONFIRM:**
- **Website and email.** The DPDP brochure uses `zorixx.ai` / `info@zorixx.ai`;
  the stock-broking brochure uses `zorixx.com` / `info@zorixx.com`. The page,
  the banner and every brochure should use one.
- **Empanelment wording.** The company brochure says "System auditor
  empanelment with NSE, BSE, MSE, MCX and NCDEX". The stock-broking brochure
  says the empanelment is "through group companies" with a scope footnote. If
  the group-company wording is the accurate one, change the bullet to
  "Empanelled with NSE, BSE, MSE, MCX and NCDEX through our group companies".
- **CERT-In.** Keep "through our partner". Zorixx itself should not be
  described as CERT-In-empanelled.
